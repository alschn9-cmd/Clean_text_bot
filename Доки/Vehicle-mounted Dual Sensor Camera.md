# Vehicle-mounted Dual Sensor Camera

## Features

- Adopting continuous zoom thermal imaging technology, it has strong ability to penetrate haze and has a long detection distance. It can search over a large area and observe at close range;
- Day and night 2 million low-light color camera, combined with thermal imaging for detailed observation, can realize continuous monitoring day and night;
- Multi-position intelligent recognition automatic focusing technology, which can realize fast focusing through a variety of focusing methods and triggering methods;
- The control software of the front-end equipment can realize online upgrade function through the network, which greatly facilitates later maintenance;
- Rich network protocols and good network adaptability, which can adapt to various network environments;
- Universal variable speed pan-tilt head realizes all-round blind-angle monitoring and high-precision positioning;
- High degree of intelligence, simple operation, easy to use and maintain;
- Special reinforcement design, suitable for various vehicles, ships and other carriers;
- Low power consumption design of the whole machine, high reliability;
- Beautiful appearance, solid structure, strong environmental adaptability.
