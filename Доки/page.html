What is EO/IR?

●Electro-Optical/Infrared (EO/IR) technology integrates two powerful imaging methods into one system.

●Electro-Optical (EO): Functions like a high-definition camera, capturing images in the visible light spectrum to provide sharp detail and ●object recognition when lighting conditions are favorable.

●Infrared (IR): Detects heat energy emitted by all objects, enabling vision in total darkness and through smoke, haze, or rain by highlighting temperature differences.

 

How EO/IR Systems Operate？

●Sensing: EO sensors collect visual imagery while IR sensors detect thermal radiation.

●Integration: The system merges both data streams, combining visible details with heat signatures.

●Output: Operators receive a unified, real-time image, delivering actionable insights in critical environments.

FAQ

 

1. What is an EO/IR system and how does it work?
An EO/IR system combines Electro-Optical (EO) sensors and Infrared (IR) sensors. The EO sensor captures high-resolution imagery in visible light, while the IR sensor detects thermal radiation emitted by objects. Together, they provide clear visuals in both day and night conditions, as well as through obscurants like fog, smoke, or rain.

 

2. What are the main applications of EO/IR systems?
EO/IR systems are widely used for surveillance, reconnaissance, border security, search and rescue, maritime monitoring, and drone operations. They allow operators to detect, identify, and track targets in various environments and lighting conditions.

 

3. What is the range and resolution of EO/IR systems?
The range and resolution vary by model. Typically, high-end EO/IR systems can detect human-sized targets at several kilometers and provide HD imagery. Many systems also support zoom and tracking functions for precise identification.


4. Can EO/IR systems operate in complete darkness or adverse weather?
Yes. The infrared component allows the system to detect thermal signatures in total darkness, smoke, fog, or light rain. However, heavy precipitation or extreme weather may slightly reduce detection performance.


5. How is the EO/IR system mounted or integrated?
EO/IR systems can be mounted on drones, vehicles, ships, or stationary platforms. Many systems feature gimbals for 360° rotation, stabilization, and automatic target tracking, ensuring clear imagery even on moving platforms.

 

6. What maintenance or operational support is required for EO/IR systems?
Regular cleaning of lenses, firmware updates, and periodic calibration are recommended. Most modern EO/IR systems are designed for low-maintenance operation and come with technical support and training from the manufacturer
