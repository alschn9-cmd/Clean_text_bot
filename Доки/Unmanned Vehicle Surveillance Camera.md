# Unmanned Vehicle Surveillance Camera

**Features:** Gyro Stabilization, LRF (Laser Ranger Finder), 360 Degree Coverage, High Reliability, Day & Night, AI Solution, IP66 Waterproof with Military Connectors, Low Maintenance. Optional colors available.

## Key Features:

- Can integrate HD visible light and uncooled infrared thermal imagers;
- Rotation speed can reach over 100°/s, and can track fast-moving targets;
- Azimuth rotation range 0°～360°, pitch rotation range -90°～+90°;
- Supports area intrusion alarm, boundary crossing alarm, motion detection, target recognition, automatic tracking, and radar linkage.
